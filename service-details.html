Build a Clearer Understanding of Biological Science

Gladys Nsikanabasi Ikara has a B.Sc. in Biology and is pursuing an M.Sc. in Biotechnology at the Nigerian Defence Academy. Her academic and laboratory experience informs a learning approach focused on clear explanations, scientific reasoning, and careful use of evidence.

Learning support can be arranged around the learner's level and topics. Sessions may focus on understanding core concepts, reviewing class material, preparing for assessments, and learning how to approach scientific questions systematically.

Topics That Can Be Covered

Biology Fundamentals

Support with key biological concepts, terminology, and the relationships between biological systems.

Biotechnology Foundations

Introductory explanations of biotechnology concepts and their applications in research and health.

Molecular Biology

Study support for molecular biology topics using clear explanations and structured revision.

Scientific Study Skills

Guidance on organizing notes, understanding scientific texts, and documenting research clearly.

A Structured, Learner-Focused Approach

Sessions can begin by identifying the learner's topic and current level, then breaking difficult ideas into manageable steps. The focus is on understanding the material rather than memorizing isolated definitions.

The topics, learning format, schedule, and fees can be discussed directly before a session is arranged.
